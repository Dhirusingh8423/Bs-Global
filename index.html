Our school

Rooted in community. Focused on possibility.

At Bhisham Singh Inter College, education is more than lessons in a classroom. It is the steady work of building understanding, confidence, responsibility and respect.

Located in Pipra Baghel, Deoria, our school welcomes families who want a supportive place for their children to learn and prepare for the next step in life.

“A strong education opens doors—and gives students the confidence to walk through them.”Our belief
